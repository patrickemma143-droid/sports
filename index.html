DATA RULES

What qualifies as a signal

01Know when it was known

Every injury, roster, odds, or public update needs a timestamp before kickoff.

02Separate evidence from opinion

Posts can flag context; sentiment does not become a strength rating without evidence.

03Keep source terms attached

Respect provider limits, platform terms, retention, and redistribution rules.

04Prove performance forward

Compare against simple baselines across future seasons, then report calibration and misses.
